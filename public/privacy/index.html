3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to deliver and personalize the lesson path; personalize Penny AI responses; display lesson progress; send profile data to the Anthropic Claude API for Penny responses; manage subscription billing through Stripe; send service-related emails; send SMS marketing to opted-in subscribers; analyze usage patterns; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Personality data — special notice

Personality data (MBTI type, Western astrology sign, Chinese zodiac sign) is optional, used exclusively to personalize your Habitrii experience, sent to Anthropic only when you interact with Penny, not shared with third parties for advertising or profiling, and provided for entertainment and self-reflection only.

6. SMS text messaging

Habitrii offers optional SMS marketing communications. By opting in, you agree to receive text messages including money mindset tips, product updates, and promotional content.

Opt-in: Subscribe via sign-up forms or by texting a keyword to our designated number. Consent to SMS is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Frequency: Message frequency varies.

Message & data rates: Standard rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.

Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation text and no further messages will be sent.

Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or email mail@aven4life.com.

Your mobile phone number will not be shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.

8. Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, secure environment variable storage for API keys, and industry-standard security practices from our third-party service providers.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at mail@aven4life.com.

9. Your rights

9.1 All users

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable, readily usable format
  • Opt-out — Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

9.2 Virginia residents (VCDPA)

Virginia residents may confirm whether we process their personal data, access it, correct inaccuracies, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising or data sales (we do not currently engage in these practices). Email mail@aven4life.com with subject “Virginia Privacy Request.” Appeals may be submitted with subject “Virginia Privacy Appeal.”

9.3 California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents may know what personal information we collect and how we use it; delete it (subject to exceptions); correct inaccurate data; opt out of data sales (we do not sell personal information); and receive non-discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights. Email mail@aven4life.com with subject “CCPA Privacy Request.”

9.4 EU / EEA residents (GDPR)

EU/EEA residents have rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), and consent withdrawal. Our legal bases: contract performance, legitimate interests, and consent for optional personality data. Email mail@aven4life.com with subject “GDPR Privacy Request” for a response within 30 days.

10. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain session state, remember preferences and progress, and collect analytics to improve the Service. Types: Essential (required for functionality) and Analytics. You may control cookies through your browser, though disabling essential cookies may prevent the Service from functioning correctly.

11. Children’s privacy — strict 18+ requirement

Habitrii is exclusively for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not permit use by minors under any circumstances, including with parental or guardian consent. If we learn we have inadvertently collected data from a person under 18, we will immediately suspend access, delete all personal information, and take steps to prevent future access. Contact mail@aven4life.com if you believe we have collected information from a minor.

12. Links to third-party sites

The Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

13. International data transfers

Habitrii is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. For EU/EEA users, transfers are made pursuant to appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

14. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the revised policy with an updated date and sending an email notification. Your continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
